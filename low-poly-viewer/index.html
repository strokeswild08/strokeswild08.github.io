New to 3D? A quick guide ＋

Rotate & zoom. Drag the model with your mouse. Scroll to move closer. On touch screens, use one finger to orbit and pinch to zoom. Focus the preview to use arrow keys, +/− and R.

Bring your own. In Blender, use File → Export → glTF 2.0 and choose GLB. Export with embedded textures and without Draco or Meshopt compression. This viewer accepts a single self-contained .glb, up to 25 MB.

Inspect the geometry. Wireframe reveals triangle edges. The count is the model’s base triangle count before skinning or modifiers. Preview materials use your model’s original shading.
