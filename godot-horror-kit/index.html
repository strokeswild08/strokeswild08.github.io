01 / ENEMY AI

It hears you running.

Patrol, chase and search states. Wall-occluded sight, sprint detection and grid routes around the station.

02 / INTERACTION

Every object has a purpose.

Read notes, collect a brass key, recharge your flashlight and unlock the exit with camera-ray interactions.

03 / PERSISTENCE

Pick up where you left off.

Validated checkpoints restore your location, key and flashlight. Pause, retry and finish a complete shift.

GET INTO THE STATION

Open it. Play it. Make it yours.

  1. Download the project ZIP and extract it.
  2. Import project.godot in Godot 4.4.1.
  3. Press F5 in the editor, then choose Start a new shift.
  4. Find the archive key in the south-west and reach the north-east exit.
WASD · MoveMouse · LookShift · SprintE · InteractF · FlashlightEsc · Pause
