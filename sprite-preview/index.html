How to use it ＋

Start with the frame size. Open a static PNG or WebP sheet, then enter the width and height of one frame. Sheets are read left to right, one animation row at a time, without padding between cells.

Choose a clip. Choose one of the eight actions, or select a row and its first/last frame. Tune the FPS or try ping-pong. Space plays/pauses; arrow keys step through the clip when the preview has focus.

Export the motion. PNG saves the current frame; GIF saves the selected clip, loop and speed; Play once exports a non-looping GIF. GIF uses up to 256 colors and 1-bit transparency. Timing is rounded to 10 ms steps.
